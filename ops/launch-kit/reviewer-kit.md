## Screen the reviewer in a 15-minute call

1. Ask about two recent civilian service RFQs they reviewed. Accept redacted descriptions if clients are confidential.
2. Ask how they find obligations that an AI requirement list missed.
3. Ask how they check amended deadlines, required forms, page limits, and reference details.
4. Ask how they separate an unsupported fact from an approved future commitment.
5. Ask for one reference and permission to contact that person. A credential alone does not establish competence.
6. Confirm they can work independently of our draft-generation process.
7. Confirm confidentiality, any conflict with competing bidders, secure file handling, and deletion after the agreed retention period.
8. Confirm the fixed fee includes checking corrected findings and recording the final version reviewed.

Choose someone who gives source-based examples. Do not select solely on claimed win rate. Keep a second candidate available.

**Decision:** Accept / Accept after listed corrections / Cannot accept. List any area you could not verify. An unresolved mandatory issue prevents acceptance.

**Timing:** [first review date/time zone]; [correction check date/time zone]. Agree these dates before work.

**Fee:** [fixed amount and currency]. **Payment:** [agreed milestone]. No extra work without a new written scope and price.

**Amendments:** Include amendments present at the agreed source freeze. Review later amendments for scope impact before committing to a new date or fee.

**Independence:** No contingent payment for an acceptance decision or government award. No guarantee of an award or agency acceptance.

**Authorization:** Work starts only after Jefferson sends a written work order. A quote request or source inspection does not authorize paid work.

## Reviewer handoff files

Send the original source package, the version manifest, draft DOCX/PDF, compliance matrix, evidence index, supplied price sheet, required forms, and unresolved questions. Use a private folder. Limit access to the named reviewer. Do not send a customer package before the customer approves that sharing.

Use [the findings CSV](templates/reviewer-findings.csv). Record the reviewer name, date, revision, and file hashes with the returned decision. A general “looks good” email is insufficient.
