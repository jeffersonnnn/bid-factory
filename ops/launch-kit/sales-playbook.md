# Find and sell the first pilot

## Who to approach

Start with an established small janitorial contractor that has comparable work, available references, and a live civilian RFQ. Prefer an owner or operations lead who prepares bids without a dedicated proposal team.

Avoid the first pilot if the contractor lacks required eligibility, cannot price the work, missed a mandatory visit, or cannot supply records. Avoid complex multi-volume, defense, classified, controlled-information, or unusual-security packages in V0.

Use [the prospect seed list](templates/prospect-seeds.csv) for initial research. Those companies advertise relevant services. They are not confirmed buyers, verified eligible offerors, or matched prospects yet.

## Build the list of 20

1. Search [SAM.gov Contract Opportunities](https://sam.gov/opportunities) for active civilian janitorial solicitations.
2. Filter for janitorial services, typically NAICS 561720, and a location the contractor can serve.
3. Choose an actual solicitation. Do not treat a sources-sought notice as a bid invitation.
4. Check the deadline, time zone, amendments, mandatory visits, set-aside, instructions, and evaluation factors in the original files.
5. Require at least ten business days before the deadline for the first pilot, unless a complete shorter schedule is explicitly agreed.
6. Search [USAspending](https://www.usaspending.gov/search) for comparable janitorial contract recipients in that area.
7. Find the contractor's own website and public business contact route.
8. Record one evidence-based fit reason, the source link, and the verification date.
9. Confirm current registration and required eligibility before accepting the work. A website claim is insufficient.
10. Contact five qualified prospects first. Improve the message from their replies before contacting the next five.

SAM.gov allows opportunity searches without an account. An account adds saved searches and change-following tools. [Source](https://sam.gov/opportunities).

## The useful demonstration

Prepare a one-page opportunity note for each prospect:

- Opportunity number, official link, agency, location, deadline, and last amendment checked.
- Two or three submission obligations with exact source pages.
- One plausible fit reason from the contractor's public website, clearly marked as unconfirmed.
- Two questions only the contractor can answer.
- Our scope, fee, and proposed delivery date, subject to intake and reviewer availability.

Do not claim you found an error in a contractor's proposal unless you actually received and reviewed that proposal. Public award data does not contain their private bid.

[Official opportunity link]

Jefferson

## A short manual call opening

“Hello, this is Jefferson from [business]. We help janitorial contractors prepare federal bid responses. Are you the person who handles bids?”

If yes: “I found [RFQ] in [location]. It requires [specific item]. Are you considering it?”

If interested: “We can show you a short example. The pilot is $1,500, with $750 to start after a fit check.”

If no: Thank them. Record the preference. Do not continue the sequence.

## Eight-minute demonstration

| Minute | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 | The public RFQ or clearly fictional sample | “This example shows the process. These sample company facts are fictional.” |
| 1–2 | A requirement and its exact source | “Each requirement keeps its source location.” |
| 2–3 | Company evidence and a mapped claim | “We use your documents and confirmed answers.” |
| 3–4 | A missing information question | “A missing fact creates a question. It does not create an invented answer.” |
| 4–5 | Pricing blocked without customer input | “You provide and approve the price.” |
| 5–6 | Draft response and internal compliance matrix | “You can see the response and what supports it.” |
| 6–7 | Review findings and version record | “An independent specialist reviews your actual package before final delivery.” |
| 7–8 | Offer and intake checklist | “If this RFQ fits, we confirm the scope and review slot, then request the $750 deposit.” |

Use screen sharing. The app runs on your computer. Do not send the localhost URL as a customer portal.

## Five qualification questions

1. Which RFQ are you considering, and have you downloaded all amendments?
2. Can you meet its eligibility, location, staffing, and site-visit requirements?
3. Which comparable projects and references can you document?
4. Who will provide the prices and approve the final commitments?
5. Can that person return our questions within one business day?

If any mandatory condition is impossible, stop before taking payment. If evidence is missing but obtainable, agree the intake deadline.

## The close

“Based on this fit check, the proposed scope is [scope]. The fee is $1,500. The deposit is $750. I will confirm the reviewer slot and delivery date in the order form. Once you accept and the funds clear, we start.”

Do not promise an award, an agency acceptance decision, or a completed review that has not happened.

## Where Monid fits

Use Monid for research and administrative follow-up once its available tools, cost, and authorization are confirmed. We have not connected Monid or launched a campaign.

Start with personal email and manual calls. Do not treat the Monid video as permission to run AI cold calls. AI-generated voices fall within the FCC's artificial/prerecorded-voice rules; applicable consent requirements depend on the call and recipient. Use AI voice follow-up only after the required consent and calling setup are confirmed. [FCC ruling](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-24-17A1.pdf).

Commercial email rules also apply to business-to-business email. Use truthful sender details and subjects, identify the commercial purpose, include a valid postal address and opt-out, and honor opt-outs within ten business days. [FTC guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business).

## Record after each conversation

Record fit, objection, next action, date, permission preference, and whether a deposit is pending or cleared. Use a small local table. The app does not need a CRM.
