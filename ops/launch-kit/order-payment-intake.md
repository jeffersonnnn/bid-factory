## Payment procedure

1. Confirm the payment account belongs to the stated seller.
2. Confirm international payment support, currency conversion, fees, withdrawal access, and payout timing in that account.
3. Complete the order form and obtain written acceptance.
4. Issue the deposit invoice through the existing business account or its invoice tool.
5. Confirm payment in the account itself. Do not rely on an emailed receipt or screenshot.
6. Wait for available funds before committing money to the reviewer.
7. Reserve the quoted reviewer fee and other direct costs.
8. Keep uncommitted funds available for corrections and refunds. Cleared funds can still face disputes.
9. Obtain customer acceptance of the reviewed response.
10. Issue the balance invoice and confirm available funds.
11. Release the final package before the agreed handoff deadline.
12. Record the payment dates, fees, refunds, and reviewer payment.

## Complete intake checklist

Request one private folder with these items:

- Full solicitation, PWS/SOW, instructions, evaluation criteria, forms, attachments, wage information where supplied, questions and answers, and every amendment.
- Official source link, government deadline and time zone, submission destination, and any site-visit or question deadlines.
- Company legal name, address, UEI, CAGE where required, contact, and named approver.
- Evidence for the required eligibility and certifications, including expiry dates.
- Capability statement and relevant company policies or procedures.
- The required number of comparable references, with scope, dates, customer details, and other fields required by this RFQ.
- Required resumes, qualifications, licenses, and proposed staffing commitments.
- The customer's completed pricing schedule, assumptions, quantities, and named price approval.
- Required signed forms and amendment acknowledgments.
- Confirmation that the customer may share the documents with us, the model services, and the named reviewer.
- A contact who can resolve missing facts within one business day.

Record missing items as questions. Do not invent answers to complete intake. Do not request SAM.gov passwords or submit on the customer's behalf.
