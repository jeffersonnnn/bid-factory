# Bid Factory: first paid pilot

Prepared for Jefferson. Working brand: Bid Factory. Date: September 23, 2026.

## The offer

Prepare one simple civilian federal janitorial RFQ response for **$1,500 USD**. Collect **$750 before work**. Collect **$750 after acceptance and before releasing the final editable package**. Send a review PDF before acceptance. State all applicable taxes before agreement.

The customer supplies prices and confirms all company facts. An independent federal proposal specialist reviews the response. The customer submits it.

These are proposed pilot terms. No customer or reviewer has accepted them. No messages, invoices, charges, or hiring requests have been sent.

## The next five actions

1. Send the quote request to three reviewer candidates. Request a written price and availability. Do not order work.
2. Fill the seller name, business address, registration country, and payment method in the order form.
3. Select one active civilian janitorial RFQ with enough time for intake, review, and corrections.
4. Match five contractors to that opportunity. Verify their service area and eligibility before outreach.
5. Demonstrate the source-to-response workflow. Offer one pilot after the fit check.

Aim for 20 qualified prospects, five conversations, and one deposit. These are targets, not forecasts.

## Before accepting a deposit

- Confirm a reviewer, fixed fee, correction check, and available review slot for the actual document package.
- Confirm the RFQ fits the scope limits. Confirm the customer can meet any required site visit and eligibility conditions.
- Confirm the final delivery date precedes the government deadline by at least one business day.
- Agree the order form, cancellation terms, data use, and customer responsibilities.
- Confirm the payment account can receive this customer's payment and release funds before reviewer work begins.
- Ensure the net deposit covers all committed cash costs. Do not count an invoice or pending payout as available money.

You can demonstrate the product before these items are complete. Do not promise a fixed delivery date until they are complete.

## Cash rule

Example only: $750 deposit minus $400 reviewer fee minus $50 model/processing allowance leaves $300 uncommitted. The allowance is a budget, not an observed cost. Keep uncommitted funds available for corrections and refunds. This is not profit.

Set a target review price of $400 and a provisional ceiling of $500, including one correction check. A candidate may quote more. Increase the deposit, change scope by agreement, or decline the bid if the deposit cannot cover costs. Never promise a reviewer fee before a quote.

## What is still yours to choose

- Legal seller name, business country, business address, and customer-facing contact details.
- Existing payment account and settlement currency.
- Reviewer selection and the first customer's real solicitation.

The kit uses explicit placeholders for these items. No payment link exists yet.

## Product limits

The app is a local operator tool. Demonstrate it by screen sharing. The localhost link does not work on a customer's computer. Use a private file exchange for customer documents. A hosted customer portal is not required for the first service engagement.

The sample uses fictional company records and a synthetic RFQ. It is not an award, an expert endorsement, or a real government submission. Live-model test results appear in the separate verification note.
