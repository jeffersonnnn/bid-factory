# First-client acquisition funnel

Prepared September 23, 2026. This is the supervised V0 workflow.

## The entry offer

Do not open with “buy proposal software.” Open with one useful result:

> We found a live federal janitorial RFQ that may fit your company. We mapped its mandatory submission requirements against your public capability information. Here are the confirmed matches, missing proof, and questions to answer before you decide to bid.

Call this the **Bid Readiness Scan**. Keep it to one page. Use only public facts. Mark every contractor fact as public, confirmed, or unknown. Do not claim to have reviewed a private proposal.

## The first-client path

### 1. Select one opportunity

Choose one active civilian janitorial RFQ from the official SAM.gov record. Confirm the notice type, deadline, time zone, amendment list, set-aside, service location, site visit and submission method.

Require at least ten business days before the deadline for the first pilot. Reject an opportunity when a mandatory site visit has passed.

### 2. Select five contractors

Match public contractor information against service area, NAICS, set-aside and past-performance needs. Keep public-source links and the date checked.

Do not accept a website claim as final eligibility evidence. Confirm registration and certifications during intake.

### 3. Generate a one-page scan for each contractor

Include:

- The official opportunity link, agency, location and deadline.
- Three important submission obligations with exact source pages.
- Public contractor evidence that appears relevant.
- Three missing or unconfirmed items.
- A short invitation to review the scan.

Example:

> The RFQ requires three comparable references, a completed price sheet and a five-page technical response. Your website shows federal janitorial work, but it does not provide three complete reference records. I can show you the full requirement map in 15 minutes.

### 4. Send compliant cold email

Send the scan through a personal business email. Use a truthful subject, sender identity, commercial-purpose disclosure, postal address and working opt-out.

Send one follow-up after two or three business days. Stop after an opt-out.

Do not send cold automated texts. Do not start an AI-generated sales call without the consent required for that call and number.

### 5. Capture interest and calling permission

Send interested prospects to a short form. Collect:

- Name, company, email and telephone number.
- The RFQ they want to discuss.
- Service location and decision-maker role.
- Preferred meeting time.
- Separate, recorded permission for AI-generated marketing calls and text messages where applicable.

Store the permission text, timestamp, source page and number. Let the person continue by email without telephone consent.

### 6. Let Monid qualify and book the interested lead

Use the AI call after the prospect requests the call and the required permission exists. The agent should identify the seller and disclose that it is an AI assistant.

Ask only these questions:

1. Are you considering this RFQ?
2. Can you perform in the required location?
3. Can you meet the stated set-aside and mandatory conditions?
4. Do you have the required comparable references?
5. Who controls pricing and final approval?
6. Can your team return missing information within one business day?

Book a 15-minute demonstration when the answers show possible fit. Send reminders only through permitted channels. Route uncertain answers to Jefferson. Do not let the call agent promise eligibility, delivery, compliance, an award or a price change.

### 7. Demonstrate their specific opportunity

Show the original RFQ, requirement ledger, public-evidence gaps and targeted questions. Then show the fictional proposal only to explain the later output.

State that the public scan is preliminary. State that company facts, prices and commitments require customer confirmation.

### 8. Run the paid fit gate

Before requesting payment, confirm:

- The opportunity fits V0 limits.
- The customer can meet all known mandatory conditions.
- The source package is complete.
- A reviewer gave a fixed quote and reserved a slot.
- The delivery date leaves at least one business day before the deadline.
- The $750 deposit covers committed cash costs.

Send the order and deposit invoice only after these checks pass.

### 9. Collect the deposit and intake

Wait until the $750 deposit becomes available. Then open the private intake folder and send the checklist.

Do not commission the reviewer before the funds become available. Do not count a pending payout as available money.

### 10. Deliver and close

Run the app workflow, separate-model audit, independent review, corrections and customer approval. Collect the remaining $750 before releasing the final editable package.

The customer submits the package and keeps the receipt.

## Automation states

| State | Automatic action | Human gate |
|---|---|---|
| Opportunity found | Extract requirements and create a short opportunity record. | Approve the opportunity and source completeness. |
| Contractor matched | Draft the public-evidence scan and outreach message. | Approve the contractor match and factual claims. |
| Cold email sent | Schedule one follow-up and record opt-outs. | Review replies that show interest or uncertainty. |
| Interest recorded | Collect call permission and qualification details. | Confirm permission rules and lead ownership. |
| Call permitted | Run the AI qualification call, book the demonstration and send allowed reminders. | Handle unusual questions and promises. |
| Demonstration complete | Prepare the order, deposit invoice and intake link. | Confirm fit, reviewer price and delivery date. |
| Deposit available | Start intake, prepare the package and commission the reviewer. | Approve spending and source completeness. |
| Review returned | Record findings and generate customer questions. | Correct findings and approve the exact revision. |
| Customer accepts | Send the balance invoice. | Confirm available funds and final release. |

## First-client timing

| Day | Expected event |
|---|---|
| 0 | Select one RFQ, five contractors and prepare five scans. |
| 1 | Send five emails. Make manual live calls where allowed. |
| 2–4 | Send one follow-up. Run requested AI calls. Book demonstrations. |
| 3–7 | Hold demonstrations and seek one accepted pilot order. |
| After payment | Start the five-business-day delivery schedule. |

The first sale can take longer. The schedule is a target, not a forecast.

## V0 operating limits

Run only five leads in the first batch. Listen to or review every call record. Approve every personalized scan before sending it. Record every objection and question.

Increase automation after the messages, qualification rules and handoff steps work with real prospects. Do not scale a script that has not produced one good customer conversation.

## What still needs a connection

- A Monid API key and verified access to the calling workflow.
- A sending domain, business mailbox, postal address and opt-out process.
- A short interest and consent form.
- A calendar booking link.
- A verified business invoice and payment account.
- A private document intake folder.
- A reviewer with a written quote and available review slot.

No automated campaign has started. No calls, emails or text messages have been sent.
