# Delivery from deposit to final package

## Before work starts

- Record the accepted order, cleared deposit, net available funds, confirmed reviewer quote, and review slot.
- Record the complete source manifest and amendment sequence. Check the official opportunity again before starting.
- Agree the customer answer deadline, review slot, balance-payment time, and final handoff time.
- Confirm permission for model processing and reviewer access.

## Day 1: extract and identify gaps

1. Upload all original source files and amendments.
2. Extract requirements through OpenRouter.
3. Compare every source page with the extracted list. Add omissions using exact source quotes.
4. Confirm atomic obligations, mandatory flags, reference counts, attachments, page limits, deadline time zone, and amendment lineage.
5. Extract company evidence. Keep complete reference details together.
6. Reject unsupported, stale, or conflicting evidence. Preserve the review record.
7. Approve sufficient evidence for each requirement.
8. Send the missing-information questions to the named customer contact.

## Day 2: prepare the review package

1. Record named customer answers and price approval.
2. Link required price sheets, signed forms, and other attachments to the matching requirements.
3. Generate the evidence-only draft.
4. Inspect the actual DOCX and PDF. Check text, tables, pagination, required signatures, and attachment filenames.
5. Run the separate AI audit. Resolve its findings without treating it as human expertise.
6. Send the full reviewer handoff bundle through the agreed private channel.

## Days 3–4: independent review and correction

1. Obtain the source-based finding log from the specialist.
2. Record every finding in the app. Record omissions even if the app did not extract them.
3. Ask the customer for any missing facts or commitments.
4. Correct the evidence, mappings, source settings, or response.
5. Regenerate the draft after changes. Earlier approval does not cover a changed version.
6. Return the corrected files and finding log to the reviewer.
7. Obtain a written correction decision for the exact version.

A promised turnaround is subject to the actual reviewer slot and timely customer answers. If a correction needs more time, communicate a new schedule before the deadline.

## Day 5: acceptance, payment, and handoff

1. Check the official opportunity for new amendments or deadline changes.
2. Process any new amendment before approval. Recheck affected facts and requirements.
3. Confirm all mandatory findings are closed.
4. Run the final audit against the current draft.
5. Obtain the customer's approval of facts, prices, staffing, commitments, and the review PDF.
6. Record the named reviewer and customer decisions, dates, exact revision, and file hashes.
7. Collect the balance under the accepted order.
8. Approve the exact package in the app only when all blockers are resolved.
9. Export the final DOCX/PDF package and inspect the output files again.
10. Provide only the submission documents to the customer for submission. Identify the internal records separately.
11. Ask the customer to submit through the required channel and keep the receipt.
12. Confirm the agreed retention or deletion date for working documents.

## Which files go where

| Folder | Contents | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Submission | Final proposal, customer-approved price sheet, required forms, signed acknowledgments, required attachments | Customer submits only the files the solicitation requests. |
| Internal review | Compliance matrix, evidence index, finding log, source manifest, approval record | Customer/operator records. Do not include automatically in a government submission. |
| Source originals | Solicitation, amendments, company documents | Private reference archive. |

The app's ZIP includes internal review files. It is a handoff bundle, not a direction to submit every file.

## Stop conditions

Stop final delivery if a mandatory requirement is unresolved, facts conflict, the price lacks customer approval, an amendment is unprocessed, or the deadline passed. Stop if the reviewer cannot accept the final version. Never remove a blocker to meet a sales promise.

If the customer cannot supply required evidence, agree a pause, revised scope, or cancellation under the order. A deposit is not permission to invent the missing material.

## Measure the pilot

Record preparation hours, reviewer hours, customer waiting time, source omissions, unsupported-claim findings, revision count, model cost, review fee, payment fees, and net cash. Ask the customer what saved time and what remained difficult.

Do not remove expert review after one successful synthetic test or one paid bid. Use actual defect records to decide which checks can safely become automatic.
