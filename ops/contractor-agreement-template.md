# Operator and engineer contractor agreement template

This is a working template. It is not legal advice. Use local legal and tax review before signing.

The worker's legal status depends on the real working relationship. A contract label alone may not control that status.

## Parties

This agreement starts on [date] between:

- Client: [legal name, address, registration details]
- Contractor: [legal name, address, registration details]

## Services

The contractor will:

- Build, test, document, and operate Bid Factory within written assignments.
- Maintain the evidence, amendment, blocker, and approval controls.
- Prepare draft sales and delivery materials when assigned.
- Protect customer bid documents, prices, personal data, and credentials.
- Report defects, security events, deadline risks, and scope risks quickly.
- Follow the decision rights in `decision-rights.md`.

The contractor will not contact a customer, incur a cost, hire a reviewer, deploy production, or release final files without required approval.

## Deliverables and acceptance

Each assignment will state:

- The deliverable.
- The due date.
- The acceptance test.
- The price or approved hours.
- The approved expenses.
- The repository branch or file location.

The client will accept or reject a deliverable within [number] business days. A rejection must identify the failed acceptance test.

## Confidential information

Confidential information includes customer solicitations, company records, prices, references, credentials, source code, prompts, model results, reviewer findings, business plans, and nonpublic financial details.

The contractor will:

- Use confidential information only for assigned work.
- Give access only to approved people and services.
- Keep credentials out of source control, documents, logs, and chat.
- Use approved private storage and named accounts.
- Report suspected loss or access within [number] hours.
- Return or delete confidential information at the agreed time.
- Confirm deletion in writing when requested.

These duties continue for [period] after the agreement ends. Trade-secret duties continue while the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

## Customer data and security

The contractor will not accept classified information or controlled information that needs an unimplemented security standard.

The contractor will follow the approved access list, retention period, deletion process, backup process, and incident process.

The contractor will not move customer files to a new model, host, storage provider, or device without written approval.

## Intellectual property

The contractor assigns to the client all rights that the contractor creates specifically for the project, to the extent allowed by applicable law. This assignment includes source code, tests, documentation, prompts, templates, designs, and operating materials.

The contractor will sign documents reasonably needed to record this assignment. The contractor waives moral rights where the law permits a waiver.

The contractor must list preexisting tools, code, templates, and other materials in Schedule A. The contractor keeps ownership of those materials. The contractor grants the client a permanent, worldwide, royalty-free license to any listed material included in a project deliverable.

The contractor will not include third-party material unless its license allows the intended use. The contractor will record each third-party license.

If United States law applies, do not rely only on a work-made-for-hire label. The U.S. Copyright Office states that commissioned work qualifies only in limited cases. Use the express assignment above. See [Copyright Office Circular 30](https://copyright.gov/circs/circ30.pdf).

## Warranties

The contractor states that:

- The contractor can enter this agreement.
- The contractor will perform the services with reasonable care.
- The contractor will not knowingly deliver malicious code.
- The contractor will disclose known third-party licenses and conflicts.
- The contractor will not knowingly use another party's confidential information.

The project does not guarantee a government award, agency acceptance, or error-free model output.

## Independent relationship

The parties intend an independent contractor relationship, subject to applicable law.

The contractor controls the method and schedule of work within deadlines and security rules. The contractor supplies normal work tools unless an assignment states otherwise.

The contractor cannot bind the client, sign for the client, accept customer terms, or represent ownership without written authority.

If United States law applies, review the actual behavioral control, financial control, and relationship facts. See the [IRS worker classification guidance](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc762).

## Conflicts and non-solicitation

The contractor will disclose work for a competing bidder on the same opportunity.

The contractor will not use customer information to solicit that customer outside this project for [period], to the extent allowed by applicable law.

## Term and termination

Either party may end this agreement with [number] days' written notice.

The client may end it immediately for a material confidentiality, security, fraud, or access breach.

At termination, the contractor will stop access, return property, deliver completed work, identify unfinished work, and delete retained copies as required.

The client will pay accepted work and approved nonrecoverable expenses through the termination date.
